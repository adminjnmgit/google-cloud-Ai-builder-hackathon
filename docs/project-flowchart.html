How Jagran Orbit works

It reads the daily business news, spots the stories that change a small business's costs or sales, and tells the owner what that means in rupees and what to do next.

It remembers the owner

Changes are saved as soon as they are made, and come back on the next visit, even on a cleared browser.

It keeps working if the server is down

The page falls back to a saved copy of the news and the owner's last numbers, then catches up when the server returns.

It is a starting point, not advice

Figures are what-if estimates from news and the owner's own inputs. They are not forecasts or professional advice.

In one line: it is a newsroom analyst that reads every article, flags the ones that matter to your costs, and tells you what to do about them.
